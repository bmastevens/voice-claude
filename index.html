Voice Claude

A personal, voice-only assistant for working while commuting.

Voice Claude is a private tool built and used by one person. It lets its owner talk to an AI assistant through a phone and headset while driving or on the train, with no need to look at a screen.

What it does with Google

With the owner's consent, Voice Claude uses Google sign-in to:

  • read Gmail messages so they can be summarised aloud (gmail.readonly);
  • create drafts in the owner's own Gmail account for later review (gmail.compose);
  • read Google Calendar events so the day's diary can be read aloud (calendar.readonly).

Voice Claude never sends email. Drafts stay in the owner's Drafts folder until the owner reviews and sends them by hand.

Who can use it

Only its owner. Voice Claude is not offered to the public and there is no sign-up.

Privacy policy

Voice Claude is an independent personal project. It is not made or endorsed by Google or Anthropic.
